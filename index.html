FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask directly, no obligation.

Ask on WhatsApp →
How do I pay?

OMT, Whish, USDT, or bank transfer. 50% to start, 50% when you approve the preview, before going live.

How long does it take?

One-Page Site in 5 days, Full Site in 10, Custom Builds in 2–3 weeks, counted from the day we receive your content.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo (or even just the name), photos if you have them, and what you want the site to say. Design, structure, and writing are our job.

Can you make changes after launch?

Yes. Small edits like prices, photos, and text are included in your yearly fee. Bigger additions get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Do I own the website?

Fully. The domain is registered in your name and the files are yours. If you ever leave, everything comes with you.

Can you build an online store?

Yes, see Gazzello and Elegance Store above. Stores are quoted as Custom Builds since they need products and checkout. Tell us what you sell and we'll send a fixed quote.

Ready to go live?

Tell us about your business on WhatsApp. You'll get a fixed price and a delivery date, no strings attached.
