/process

How it works

Three steps, no meetings required. The whole project can run over WhatsApp.

STEP 01

Pick and send

Choose a package and send your logo, photos, and text, or just tell us about the business and we'll draft the words.

STEP 02

Review the preview

You get a private link to the real site, on your phone. Two revision rounds are included in every package.

STEP 03

Go live

We connect your domain, secure it, and hand you the keys. Updates afterward take a WhatsApp message.

/faq

Questions

How do I pay?

OMT, Whish, USDT, or bank transfer. 50% to start, 50% when you approve the preview, before going live.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo (or even just the name), photos if you have them, and what you want the site to say. Design, structure, and writing are our job.

Can you make changes after launch?

Yes. Small edits like prices, photos, and text are included in your yearly fee. Bigger additions get quoted as a fixed price before any work starts.

Do I own the website?

Fully. The domain is registered in your name and the files are yours. If you ever leave, everything comes with you.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. Stores are quoted separately as Custom Builds, since they need product management and checkout. Message us with what you sell and we'll send a fixed quote.
